# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, respectful, and safe collaboration space for all participants contributing to **AnesthAssist**, regardless of background, identity, clinical specialty, or level of software experience.

---

## Expected Standards

Participants in the AnesthAssist community are expected to:

* **Exercise Professionalism & Empathy:** Communicate with courtesy, patience, and mutual respect in all issue discussions, code reviews, and communications.
* **Focus on Patient Safety & Evidence:** Maintain an evidence-first dialogue. When discussing medication dosing, airway dimensions, or clinical procedures, ground feedback in recognized literature with transparency.
* **Respect Constructive Feedback:** Gracefully give and receive clinical and engineering critiques focused on improving the safety and usability of the application.
* **Prioritize User Privacy:** Strictly uphold the Zero-PHI principle; never post real patient information, de-identified clinical notes with re-identification risk, or hospital-internal credentials in public threads.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:

* Harassment, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
* Sharing protected health information (PHI) or confidential medical records.
* Inappropriate or disruptive commentary in issue trackers, pull requests, or project channels.
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported directly to the project lead:

* **Lead Maintainer:** Dr. Mohamed Abdelwahab ([@drabdo1990](https://github.com/drabdo1990))

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion. Maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who violate these standards.
