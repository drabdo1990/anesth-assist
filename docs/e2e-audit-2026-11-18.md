## Driving notes
- The patient bar (weight, height, age, New case) is only mounted on Pre-op and Intra-op Dosing, Airway and Vent. It is absent on Plans, Diseases, Post-op and Intra-op Lists.
- Checklist rows are `role=checkbox` buttons; the Lists view keeps several lists mounted, so filter to visible ones.

## Not covered
- Only a sample of the 110 condition plans, not every one.
- CICO and MH flows opened but not stepped through; LAST was only read, not interacted with.
- Offline/service-worker behaviour, mobile viewport (320 px), dark theme, and keyboard-only navigation.
- Clinical correctness of source values (needs a clinician, not a test).
- The repo has no automated E2E suite (no Playwright/Cypress). These flows were driven by hand and are not repeatable by CI. The global `verify` skill describes the method but this repo no longer has a feature map.
